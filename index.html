Jinfa Huang ( 黄锦发 )

Bonjour 👋 I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Rochester, advised by Prof. Jiebo Luo. Previously, I earned my master's degree from Peking University (PKU) under the supervision of Prof. Li Yuan and Prof. Jie Chen, and my bachelor's degree with honors from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

Research Interest: I study vision-language alignment for agentic AI, connecting multimodal understanding with visual generation.

🔥 Recent Focus

Visual Coding

Visual coding offers structured, editable representations, while diffusion adds flexible generation. Together, they may support controllable world models.

Exploring hybrid visual coding + diffusion as a pathway toward world models.

Helmholtz Agent

The name connects Helmholtz free energy with Hinton et al.'s wake-sleep algorithm, which we adapt into an agentic learning loop over real and imagined experience.

Using generative models to construct environments toward Multimodal RSI.
