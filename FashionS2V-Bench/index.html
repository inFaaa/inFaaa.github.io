01 / OVERVIEW

From one product photo to a video that still shows the same product

Fashion sellers want short product videos made from the photos they already have. FashionS2V-Bench gives each model one or two white background product images and a written description of the video, and then checks the result the way a buyer would: is it the same product, is anything visibly broken, does the video follow the brief, and does the product move as asked. Every model receives the same references and the same description.

Real catalog products

90 products across clothing, footwear, accessories and jewelry, shown on plain white catalog photos. 30 cases ask for two products in one video, so the model must keep each one correct and in its place.

Briefs that sellers write

Five prompt types cover cinematic selling shots, simulated live streams, side by side comparisons, try on and plain real seller style clips. Combined with the categories they form 11 prompt families of 10 cases each.

Measured, then grouped honestly

Four measured dimensions are averaged into a Score per video. The headline Win rate then compares models case by case, and models whose difference is not statistically significant share a tier.

02 / EVALUATION

Four dimensions, two numbers

Each generated video gets four scores between 0 and 1. Their mean is the video’s Score. The headline Win rate compares that Score with every other model on the same case.

ID · I6

Product identity

The product is cut out of the video frames and compared with the reference image attribute by attribute, for example color and shape. The score is the share of attributes judged the same. On two product cases a second check catches products that were swapped.

DEF

No major defect

A vision language model looks for visible defects such as a garbled logo, a floating product, broken hardware, a distorted shape or a product missing from a comparison. A clean video scores 1, a video whose worst defect is minor scores 0.5 and a video with a major defect scores 0.

CTX

Scene and action

Does the video show the requested scene, people and actions from the description? On two product cases the composition, with both products present and placed as asked, is part of this score.

MOT

Product motion

A motion gate checks that the product actually moves, turns or is handled as the brief asks, instead of a frozen frame. It counts only on the 90 cases whose prompt asks for product motion and is left out on the other 20.

Win rate, raw score and tiers

Win rate (headline, 0 to 100) is the share of the other models a model beats on the same case, with ties counting half, averaged over its cases, so 50 is the field average.

Score (0 to 100) is the raw v6 score, the mean of the four dimensions over the model’s videos, shown next to the Win rate.

Tiers. Models are sorted by v6. A model opens a new tier when its paired difference to the current tier leader, tested on the cases both completed with a Wilcoxon signed rank test, stays significant after Holm correction at 0.05. Inside a tier the order carries no meaning. The lowest tier merges the two lowest statistical tiers. The pairwise Elo from a Gemini based video arena is shown for reference only and is not part of the score.

FORMULA

v7 replaces the closed source judge with open weight Qwen judges, so anyone can reproduce the scores. On 417 held out same case pairs judged in the Gemini arena it picks the same winner in 75.8 percent of pairs against 76.6 percent for v6, a difference that is not significant, and its rank correlation with the arena Elo is 0.931. Win rate, Score, tiers and dimensions below are all recomputed with v7, and the lowest tier again merges the two lowest statistical tiers. The v6 release stays the default.

05 / KEY FINDINGS

What the board shows

  • 25

    The top tier is crowded. 25 of 51 models are statistically tied with the leader. Tier sizes are 25, 10, 5, 7 and 4.

  • T3

    Open source models trail. Every open source model sits in tier 3 or below. The best of them, VACE 14B, has a Win rate of 34.6 against 67.4 for the leader.

  • 0.794

    The score separates known groups. A random video from a commercial model outscores a random video from an open source model with probability 0.794 (AUROC).

  • 76.6%

    It agrees with pairwise judgments. On 417 held out same case video pairs judged in the Gemini arena, v6 picks the same winner in 76.6 percent of pairs, v7 in 75.8 percent and v3 in 72.4 percent.

  • 67.4

    Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash leads. It has the highest Win rate (67.4) and Score (95.2), and the highest no major defect score of all 51 models (95.5).

  • 20

    Motion is judged only where asked. 20 of the 110 Core cases ask for no product motion, so the motion gate is left out of their score.
