# Sunrise Harbor photographic reference prompts

GPT Image generated the [photographic harbor](optimized/visual-coding-harbor.webp) and [sailboat](optimized/visual-coding-sailboat.webp). The painting uses SVG shapes and brushstrokes drawn by the [harbor program](../recent-focus-harbor.js).

## visual-coding-harbor.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. A single cinematic wide landscape still for a very small interactive comparison of a line drawing and a rendered world. A quiet French harbor at dawn seen side-on from the water. The horizon, low silhouettes of distant quays, and dark low hills run across the middle, higher on the left. An apricot sun at upper right, finely broken reflections on slate teal seawater. A few very distant thin 19th century harbor cranes on the far left. Simple, beautiful atmosphere, finely textured believable photographic light with a hint of Monet's muted sunrise colors. Compose as a wide landscape, about 2:1, with clear empty open water across the entire lower half for a separately animated sailboat. Absolutely NO boats, sails, ships, people, lettering, diagrams, frames, or graphic overlays. This is the fixed background alone.

## visual-coding-sailboat.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Single transparent cutout sprite for a moving sailboat on a calm harbor scene, isolated on real transparency. Strict side-on profile of one elegant small traditional sailing dinghy heading to the right. Low slender near-black navy wooden hull, one straight central vertical mast, broad warm ivory triangular mainsail on the left and one smaller muted terracotta triangular foresail on the right. Entire boat visible and centered, litheness of a French harbor dinghy at sunrise, simple bold profile readable at thumbnail size, natural warm cloth shading with gentle texture, soft cool rim light on the hull. No people, water, reflection, wake, backdrop, shadow outside the subject, checkered pattern, labels, borders, letters or other objects. Do not include any keels protruding beneath the waterline. Frame the hull near horizontal, scene immediately below the waterline fully transparent. Crop fairly tight to the boat.
