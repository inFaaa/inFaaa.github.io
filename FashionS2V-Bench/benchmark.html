FashionS2V-Bench

How well do video models keep a fashion product true to its catalog photo while following a seller’s brief?

Task taxonomy ↓Statistics ↓Results by slice ↓Cases ↓Leaderboard ↗
01

Fashion specific task coverage

110 Core cases cover 4 product categories and 5 prompt types, forming 11 prompt families. 80 cases show one product and 30 put two products side by side.

02

Directly measured dimensions

Product identity, visible defects, scene and action adherence and product motion are scored per video and averaged with equal weight into a Score. The headline Win rate compares Scores case by case.

03

51 models, tied where tied

39 commercial models and 12 open source or earlier releases. Holm corrected paired tests group models into 5 tiers so that close scores are read as ties. Every judge is an open weight model.

Each filled cell is one prompt family with 10 cases. Click a cell to open its example case.

TASK TAXONOMY

Four categories, five briefs, eleven families

Every case pairs catalog photos of real products with a structured description. Only the description text is sent to the model, together with the reference images.

Prompt types

Single and two product cases

Single product cases test whether one item survives motion, hands and camera work. Two product cases add comparison: both items must appear, keep their own identity and stay on their side of the frame.
