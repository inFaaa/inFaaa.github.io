# Rouen Cathedral study

[The drawing code](../recent-focus-cathedral.js) constructs the painted façade from geometry and brushstrokes. The slider pairs that drawing with a [photographic interpretation](optimized/visual-coding-cathedral-real.webp). The built-in GPT Image tool created the photographic view in two steps. It made a reference painting, then used that composition to generate the stonework.

Monet painted the same west façade under different light. [National Gallery of Art collection entry](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/46654-rouen-cathedral-west-facade-sunlight).

## Reference painting prompt

Create ONE original late nineteenth-century French impressionist oil painting of a close architectural study of Rouen Cathedral’s WEST FACADE in Rouen, Normandy, recalling the Rouen Cathedral series by Claude Monet. Fully frontal and level. The crop is the cathedral STONE FACADE ITSELF from edge to edge, never the entire church and never a streetscape. The very large deeply recessed central pointed Gothic portal with nested archivolts, two smaller flanking Gothic doorways, pinnacles, the repeated Gothic tracery above, and a recognizable luminous circular rose window are the visual anchors. Show this facade in warm pale apricot and cream sunlight from left, periwinkle-blue and dusky lavender shadows in its recesses. Use clearly hand-painted broken brushstrokes, raised impasto, local flecks of color, broad legible tonal shapes. A real painting, not a filtered photo. Compose a very wide horizontal landscape panorama, ideally 3:1. IMPORTANT for composition: the central horizontal 75 percent of the image height must contain the recognizable central portal arch apex and the large rose window. Design for a final 320 by 86 banner. The central portal and the rose window dominate, the towers extend outside the top, the ground is outside the bottom. The stone crosses the entire image left and right, with no wide sky. Fixed straight-on view exactly centered, no people, foliage, signs, text, border, frame, canvas edge, second view, photography, split-screen, or diagram. Exactly one continuous image.

## Photographic reconstruction prompt

Precise aligned reconstruction. Edit the supplied image of Rouen Cathedral’s west facade into ONE natural high-resolution documentary architectural PHOTOGRAPH. The painting is a strict coordinate template: retain the identical pixel placements, dimensions, facade silhouette, crop, level straight-on viewpoint, exact outer edges of all three doorways and pinnacles, center of the rose window, exact shape and center of the main portal, individual tracery branches and horizontal moldings. Camera must not move, zoom, tilt or reframe even slightly. Preserve every architectural geometry and cut edge of the source. Change ONLY the material rendering, illumination quality and color into actual photographic reality: daylight, gently warm pale weathered limestone with neutral cool gray shading, hard fine edges carved in rock, worn carvings, real translucent dark-blue stained glass, true-to-life optical perspective. Strong, legible photo color separation from the original lavender-and-peach paint. Remove all paint or brush marks. No added windows, symmetric corrections, people, plants, sky, street, writing, borders, montage, or adjacent comparisons. Keep the same very wide 3:1 canvas. A single edge-to-edge architectural photograph.
