01 / OVERVIEW

From one product photo to a video that still shows the same product

Fashion sellers want short product videos made from the photos they already have. FashionS2V-Bench gives each model one or two white background product images and a written description of the video, and then checks the result the way a buyer would: is it the same product, is anything visibly broken, does the video follow the brief, and does the product move as asked. Every model receives the same references and the same description.

Real catalog products

90 products across clothing, footwear, accessories and jewelry, shown on plain white catalog photos. 30 cases ask for two products in one video, so the model must keep each one correct and in its place.

Briefs that sellers write

Five prompt types cover cinematic selling shots, simulated live streams, side by side comparisons, try on and plain real seller style clips. Combined with the categories they form 11 prompt families of 10 cases each.

Measured, then grouped honestly

Four measured dimensions are combined into a weighted Score per video, the headline number. Models whose difference is not statistically significant share a tier, and a case by case Win rate is shown for reference.

02 / EVALUATION

Four dimensions, two numbers

Each generated video gets four scores between 0 and 1, all from open weight judges and open measurements, so anyone can reproduce them. Their weighted mean is the video’s Score, the headline number. The Win rate, shown for reference, compares that Score with every other model on the same case.

ID

Product identity

An open weight vision language model compares the product in the video with the reference image. It first describes both and lists every difference, then judges seven attributes such as color, shape and logo text. The score is the share judged the same, averaged over both products on two product cases, and 0 when the product never appears.

DEF

No major defect

An open weight vision language model looks for visible defects such as a garbled logo, a floating product, broken hardware, a distorted shape or a product missing from a comparison. Inside the Score a clean video counts 1, a minor defect 0.5 and a major defect 0. The board column shows the share of videos without a major defect.

CTX

Scene and action

An open weight judge checks whether the video shows the requested scene, people and actions from the description. On two product cases the composition, with both products present and placed as asked, is part of this score.

MOT

Product motion

A motion gate checks that the product actually moves, turns or is handled as the brief asks, instead of a frozen frame. Point tracking measures the motion. It counts only on the 90 cases whose prompt asks for product motion and is left out on the other 20, and it is 0 when the product never appears.

Win rate, Score and tiers

Score (headline, 0 to 100) is a weighted mean of the four parts: no major defect counts 0.40 and identity, scene and motion 0.20 each. Where the prompt asks for no product motion, motion is dropped and the other weights are renormalised. A model’s Score is the mean over its videos.

Win rate (reference, 0 to 100) is the share of the other models a model beats on the same case, with ties counting half, averaged over its cases, so 50 is the field average.

Tiers. Models are sorted by Score. A model opens a new tier when its paired difference to the current tier leader, tested on the cases both completed with a Wilcoxon signed rank test, stays significant after Holm correction at 0.05. Inside a tier the order carries no meaning. The pairwise Elo from a Gemini based video arena is shown for reference only and is not part of the score.

Validation. On held out video pairs judged in the Gemini arena, the board picks the same winner in 81.6 percent of pairs at model level (435 pairs) and 73.6 percent per case (417 pairs). Its rank correlation with the arena Elo is 0.914.

05 / KEY FINDINGS

What the board shows

  • 25

    The top tier is crowded. 25 of 51 models are statistically tied with the leader. Tier sizes are 25, 8, 7, 7 and 4.

  • T3

    Open source models trail. Every open source model sits in tier 3 or below. The best of them, VACE 14B, has a Score of 78.3 against 93.5 for the first model.

  • 0.767

    The score separates known groups. A random video from a commercial model outscores a random video from an open source model with probability 0.767 (AUROC).

  • 81.6%

    It agrees with pairwise judgments. On held out video pairs judged in the Gemini arena, the board picks the same winner in 81.6 percent of pairs at model level (435 pairs) and 73.6 percent per case (417 pairs). Its rank correlation with the arena Elo is 0.914.

  • 93.5

    Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash has the top Score. It has the highest Score (93.5) and the highest share of videos without a major defect (95.5), inside a tier of 25 tied models. Bach 1.0 Pro has the highest Win rate (69.0).

  • 20

    Motion is judged only where asked. 20 of the 110 Core cases ask for no product motion, so the motion gate is left out of their score.
